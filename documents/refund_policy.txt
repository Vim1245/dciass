DCI Refund Policy

1. Customers can request a refund within 7 days of delivery.

2. The product must be unused and in its original condition.

3. Customers must provide a valid purchase invoice.

4. Damaged products can be reported within 48 hours of delivery.

5. Refunds are processed within 5 to 7 business days after approval.

6. Products without a valid invoice are not eligible for a standard refund.
